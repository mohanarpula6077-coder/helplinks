We believe everyone has the power to make a difference. HelpLink brings volunteers and organizations together to create stronger communities.

OUR STORY

Making Volunteering Easier for Everyone

Many organizations need helping hands, while many people want to contribute their time and skills. However, finding the right opportunity can be difficult.

HelpLink aims to bridge this gap through one platform where volunteers can discover opportunities and organizations can connect with people who care.
